About

Curious about data, careful with queries.

I'm Naveed Khan Pathan, and I like understanding how an app works behind the screen: how a customer, an order and a delivery are stored, linked and queried. That curiosity led me to databases.

My recent work is QuickBite, a food-delivery database built on Supabase (PostgreSQL) with six related tables, foreign keys, constraints, CRUD queries, joins and aggregate reports. I focus on being able to explain every table and query I write.

I'm looking for opportunities to learn, build and grow as a developer.
